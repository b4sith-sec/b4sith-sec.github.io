What I do

Research-led security work across mobile, web and API surfaces.

I approach every target as a research problem: map how the system actually works, form concrete attack hypotheses, and test them by hand rather than leaning on automated scans. Testing, analysis and write-up stay close to the person doing the work.

Researcher-led

Hands-on work, not a report factory.

This isn't a faceless testing pipeline. Abdul Basith K.P. personally handles the recon, the testing and the write-up for every piece of research — the same hands-on approach behind the findings and write-ups published on GitHub and Medium.

Method

How the research actually gets done.

01 / Model

Map the surface

Understand how the app, API or system actually behaves and where its trust boundaries sit — not just its documented endpoints.

02 / Test

Test by hand

Form concrete attack hypotheses and test them manually with research-grade techniques, instrumentation and custom tooling.

03 / Document

Prove & document

Record reproducible evidence and validate real impact before writing it up clearly — for a report, a CTF, or a public write-up.
